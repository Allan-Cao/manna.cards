23:1 | A PSALM OF DAVID. YHWH [is] my shepherd, I do not lack,   [para]
23:2 | He causes me to lie down in pastures of tender grass,
     | He leads me by quiet waters.
23:3 | He refreshes my soul,
     | He leads me in paths of righteousness
     | For His Name’s sake;
23:4 | Also—when I walk in a valley of death-shade,
     | I fear no evil, for You [are] with me,
     | Your rod and Your staff—they comfort me.
23:5 | You arrange a table before me,
     | In front of my adversaries,
     | You have anointed my head with oil,
     | My cup is full!
23:6 | Surely goodness and kindness pursue me
     | All the days of my life,
     | And my dwelling [is] in the house of YHWH,
     | For [the] length of [my] days!
